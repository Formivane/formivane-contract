# Global SOPs (approved, human-maintained)

These are standing rules for every agent. Learned rules live in `knowledge/rules/` (generated).

## Evidence
- Every external claim needs a source URL. No source, no claim.
- Quote requirements verbatim from the notice or tender pack. Paraphrase is not evidence.
- Eligibility is never "confirmed" by the system. Best case is "likely eligible (unverified)".

## Money
- Advertised value = the buyer's budget or ceiling. It is not revenue, and framework values are not guaranteed spend.
- Profit estimates are ranges with stated assumptions (config/fic.toml `[economics]`).
- Never present simulated or estimated figures as actual results.

## Actions only the owner takes
- Submitting tenders, registering on buyer portals, contacting buyers or prospects, signing anything,
  spending money, approving learned rules, approving proposals.

## Procurement basics (verify current details on gov.uk before relying on them)
- Find a Tender carries notices for higher-value public procurements; Contracts Finder carries lower-value
  English public-sector opportunities. Many notices point to a separate e-tendering portal
  (e.g. MultiQuote, PCS-Tender, Atamis, Delta) where the full documents and submission live.
- The Procurement Act 2023 regime applies to procurements started on or after 24 February 2025; suppliers
  generally need to register on the Central Digital Platform to take part. Check each notice's legal basis.
- Framework call-offs (G-Cloud, Digital Outcomes, other CCS agreements) are only open to suppliers already
  on that framework.

## Data protection (UK GDPR / PECR)
- Store organisation-level information only. Do not store named individuals' contact details from notices.
- No automated outreach. Sole traders and partnerships are "individual subscribers" under PECR: marketing
  email needs prior consent. Companies ("corporate subscribers") must get an opt-out in every message.
